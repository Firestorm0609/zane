 WARNING -- READ THIS

 1. This is a PERFECT-STREAK model. It assumes 20/20 callouts return
    exactly 1.5x. Any miss resets the maths hard: because you bet the
    WHOLE pot every time, a single -100% (rug / zero) at trade 10 wipes
    out everything. A single 0.5x at trade 15 erases most of the run.

    account against this number.
 4. 598 SOL of exit liquidity does not exist for these mints at
    this position size. Slippage alone breaks the model long
    before day 10.

 This is an arithmetic ceiling, not a forecast.
